03 / SETUP
Make it your node.
LoRa starts disabled. Read the saved settings, choose your parameters, then save them to the board.
No settings session
After clicking Connect, press RESET on the board within the countdown shown below (60 seconds). The board restarts, and the connection is restored automatically. Stop any serial monitor first.

    
Node settings
The board becomes a LoRa modem. Sideband, rnsd or another Reticulum host connects over USB and sets the frequency, bandwidth, spreading factor, coding rate and power itself; the board transmits nothing until it has done so. The hourly airtime limit stays active.

     
Match the peer’s frequency, bandwidth, SF and coding rate. Use settings permitted where you operate, and attach the correct antenna.

     
The TCP node connection is plaintext. Settings are stored in the device’s flash; this page does not save your password.

     

     
Settings are only sent after a setup prompt is detected.

   

  

  
01
A node, or an RNode modem.
By default reticulum-c runs the Reticulum node on your board. It answers /echo and /info and can bridge USB or Wi-Fi to LoRa. “Run as: RNode modem” makes the board a LoRa modem for Sideband or rnsd instead (short checks only; optional Bluetooth with a PIN, tested on the T-Echo only; no Sideband test yet). RNode EEPROM provisioning is not used.
02
Know what’s been tested.
“Bench tested” means a short hardware exchange on T-Echo and Supreme SX1262. Other boards have build and model coverage. Range, sustained load, and stock RNode interoperability remain unverified.
03
Keep a recovery path.
Use a data-capable USB cable. If a device will not connect, follow its BOOT / RESET instructions. UF2 and command-line firmware downloads remain available below each release.
